HUMANITARIAN SERVICE

Support for Rasuwa Flood Relief

On 26 August 2026, a devastating flood affected Rasuwa and surrounding communities, causing significant damage and hardship for many people.

Under the leadership of Mr. Vikash Bahadur Singh, the Golden ABC Academy family came together to support communities affected by devastating flood disasters. Demonstrating a strong commitment to social responsibility and humanitarian service, the Academy contributed NPR 233,521 towards flood disaster relief efforts. This collective support reflects Mr. Singh’s leadership and the Golden ABC Academy family’s dedication to standing with people in times of hardship and contributing to the well-being of the wider community.

This contribution reflects a commitment to supporting people during difficult times and extending humanitarian assistance beyond the immediate community.

Flood Relief Contribution Rs. 1,55,251

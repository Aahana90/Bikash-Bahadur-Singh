A VISION FOR NEPALGUNJ

Problems deserve practical answers.

Nepalgunj is a growing city with enormous potential. Its future depends on creating opportunities for young people while making everyday life cleaner, safer, better connected and better managed.

Building opportunities, improving everyday life.

A stronger Nepalgunj begins with practical solutions. Young people should have opportunities to learn useful skills, find employment and build businesses, while families should be able to live in clean, safe and well-managed communities.

With experience in education and community service, Vikash Bahadur Singh believes that development should focus on people first — strengthening education, creating employment opportunities, improving infrastructure and making public services more responsive to everyday needs.

OUR PRIORITIES

Eight priorities for a better Nepalgunj

Better Employment Opportunities

Nepalgunj's young population should be an advantage for the city's future. Education and skills should lead to real opportunities for work, entrepreneurship and professional growth.

  • Job-oriented skill development programs
  • Vocational and technical training
  • Career guidance for students and graduates
  • Support for youth entrepreneurship
  • Connect skilled youth with local businesses

Quality Education for Every Child

Quality education creates the foundation for employment, confidence and responsible citizenship. Every child should have access to an environment that encourages learning and practical development.

  • Strengthen quality teaching and learning
  • Encourage digital and practical education
  • Expand career and skill-based learning
  • Support teachers and educational institutions
  • Create better learning environments

A Cleaner City, A Healthier Community

Clean streets, markets and neighbourhoods are essential for public health and quality of life. Cleanliness should become a continuous city-wide responsibility.

  • Reliable household waste collection
  • Cleaner roads and public spaces
  • Waste segregation and recycling awareness
  • Cleaner markets and commercial areas
  • Community participation in sanitation

Proper Drainage & Flood Management

Waterlogging can disrupt homes, roads, schools and businesses. Drainage requires regular maintenance, better planning and attention to areas that are vulnerable during heavy rainfall.

  • Regular cleaning of existing drains
  • Improve storm-water drainage systems
  • Identify and monitor flood-prone areas
  • Complete pre-monsoon drainage maintenance
  • Improve emergency response during heavy rainfall

Safer Roads & Smarter Traffic

Roads should work for everyone — pedestrians, students, cyclists, businesses, public transport users and drivers.

  • Better traffic management at busy junctions
  • Safer pedestrian crossings
  • Regular road maintenance
  • Improved street lighting
  • Organized parking and transport areas

Support Local Businesses

Local businesses are important sources of employment and economic activity. Creating a supportive environment can help entrepreneurs build sustainable businesses.

  • Support small and local businesses
  • Entrepreneurship guidance for young people
  • Business and financial literacy workshops
  • Better market infrastructure
  • Encourage new local enterprises

Better Public Services

Public services should be accessible, transparent and responsive. Citizens should know how services work and where to raise concerns when problems occur.

  • Simple and accessible municipal services
  • Digital access to public information
  • Clear communication with citizens
  • Regular monitoring of development projects
  • Greater accountability in public work

A Greener & Resilient Nepalgunj

Development should go together with environmental responsibility. More trees, better public spaces and stronger preparedness can contribute to a healthier and more resilient city.

  • Increase tree planting across communities
  • Protect and improve public open spaces
  • Encourage community tree-planting programs
  • Improve neighbourhood environments
  • Promote climate and disaster preparedness
